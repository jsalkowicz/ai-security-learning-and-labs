## Overview

I completed the OpenAI Academy **AI Foundations** learning path as part of my transition toward AI security engineering and analysis.

This was guided training rather than an original software project. I am including it in GitHub to document the AI foundation I am building before moving into more technical AI security work.

The learning path introduced practical fundamentals for working with AI, including:

- artificial intelligence fundamentals
- large language models
- ChatGPT
- giving clear instructions
- providing useful context
- reviewing model outputs
- responsible AI use

## Why I Completed This Course

Before focusing on AI security, I want to understand the systems and workflows I will eventually be securing.

This course gave me a starting foundation for understanding how people interact with large language models and why instruction quality, context, output review, and responsible use matter.

## Security Relevance

The concepts in this course connect directly to later AI security topics.

For example:

- **Instructions and context** connect to prompt injection and instruction manipulation.
- **Reviewing model outputs** connects to improper output handling and human oversight.
- **Responsible AI use** connects to governance, risk management, and acceptable-use controls.
- **Large language model fundamentals** provide the background needed before studying RAG, agents, MCP, model security, and AI red teaming.

## Current Understanding

At this point, I understand AI security cannot be approached only as a traditional application-security problem.

AI systems introduce additional concerns around:

- model instructions
- context supplied to the model
- model-generated output
- human review
- downstream actions
- data exposure
- model behavior and limitations

This course is the first step in building the AI-specific knowledge layer on top of my existing cybersecurity, identity, data-protection, and cloud-security experience.
